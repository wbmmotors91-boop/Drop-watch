    What it can't do

    This reads Pokémon Center's own published product list, so a new product page appearing is Pokémon Center itself saying a new product exists. That's the alert, and announced preorders usually sit up for fifteen to sixty minutes.

    What it can't see is stock. Their list says a product exists, not whether you can buy it, so a restock of something already listed gives no signal, and a product page won't answer a request from a server at all. That's why nothing here is marked in stock or sold out: the honest thing is to show you what's new and let you check the price button yourself.

    It also means this list is new arrivals only. The thousand-odd products Pokémon Center already sells aren't finds, so they're watched quietly and never shown here. Everything else in the list above is for reading, not for alerts.

    Checks every 5 minutes. Not affiliated with Pokémon or Pokémon Center.
