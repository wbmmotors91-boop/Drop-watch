      What it can't do

      This reads Pokémon Center's own published product list, so a new product page appearing is Pokémon Center itself saying a new product exists. That's the alert, and announced preorders usually sit up for fifteen to sixty minutes.

      What it can't see is stock. The product list says a product exists, not whether you can buy it, and the product page that would say answers this app with a bot check rather than a page. That's why nothing here is marked in stock or sold out, and why a product that was already listed going on sale gives no signal. It keeps testing, hourly, in case that changes.

      It also means this list is new arrivals only: product pages that weren't there last time. Everything Pokémon Center was already listing when the watch started isn't a find, so it's known quietly and never shown here. That's worth knowing about a product you saw go on sale without an alert: if its page was up beforehand, there was no new page to catch, only a price you couldn't see.
